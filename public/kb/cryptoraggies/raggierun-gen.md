What is RaggieRun?
    RaggieRun is our first game within the CryptoRaggies project. As a matter of fact, it is the very first playable Cardano-based game to read wallets and integrate NFT assets  - something we're really proud of! RaggieRun is an arcade-style run and jump game. Your Raggie NFTs are fully integrated into the game and different traits will grant players different benefits.

How to play RaggieRun?
    RaggieRun is available on PC/Mac via browser, or as a native mobile app via Google Play Store or iOS App Store. You can visit https://cryptoraggies.io/raggierun.html to find the links to all 3 options.

How do I connect my wallet to RaggieRun?
    Currently RaggieRun supports 3 wallets - Nami, Eternl and RaggiePurrse. Once RaggieRun loads up, you can click on the icon for your favourite wallet and follow the prompts to connect.

I am connecting to RaggieRun on mobile. What is RaggieCode?
    RaggieCode is a legacy system we created in the past to enable proof of ownership on mobile before we had RaggiePurrse as a wallet connection solution. We recommend using RaggiePurrse for playing RaggieRun on mobile. However, if you prefer not to use RaggiePurrse and would like to log in using RaggieCode, please follow these steps:
        1. Navigate to our website on a desktop browser, or via a mobile dApp browser such as Flint or Vespr
        2. Click the Member Zone link from the side navigation bar (on desktop) or from the 3-dash drop down menu (on mobile)
        3. Ensure your wallet is connected to the site using the wallet connection button at the top right
        4. Once your wallet is connected, click the pink button that says Get RaggieCode
        5. This will trigger your wallet to request a signature to sign a message. Sign the message and you will be given your RaggieCode
    Please note that the RaggieCode is only valid for 5 minutes from the time of generation. You must use the RaggieCode to log in within 5 minutes or you will need a new one. RaggieCode is also case-sensitive and must be entered into RaggieRun as it is presented. The most common reasons users fail to log in using RaggieCode are because of typos, or a trailing line break or white space at the end of the RaggieCode.

I have successfully connected my wallet to RaggieRun but I am unable to use RaggieRage or RaggieRush.
    RaggieRage and RaggieRush are superpowers that are only available to holders of Raggies that have these superpower traits. If your Raggie has a trait that enables a superpower, the corresponding icon will appear below the RaggieRage or RaggieRush power bar. Once the power bar is filled and glowing, you will be able to use the superpower 3 times. After using up your superpower allowance, the power bar will reset and you will have to build it up again in order to use superpowers again. If your Raggie has more than 1 superpower under either RaggieRage or RaggieRush category, then you can toggle between them. On desktop, you can toggle RaggieRage by using the Q and W keys, and you can toggle RaggieRush by using the left and right keys. On mobile you can pick the superpower you want to use by tapping its icon underneath the power bars. Once the desired superpower has been selected and you have available allowance to use it, you can unleash your RaggieRage or RaggieRush superpower on desktop by pressing the 1 or 2 key, respectively. On mobile, the selected superpower will appear on the right side of the screen. Simply tap on it to use it!

What is RaggieRage?
    RaggieRage is a collection of Raggie superpowers that come with some of the rare Raggie traits! Within RaggieRun, usage of RaggieRage superpowers is controlled by the RaggieRage bar. In order to fill this bar, you'll need to collect items in the game. Collecting any items or destroying any point-awarding obstacles in the game will fill the bar. Once the bar reaches a threshold, a countdown will start and a small amount will start to drain from the bar every 10 seconds until the minimum threshold is reached. This timer will reset every time RaggieRage goes up so keep collecting items to prevent it from falling! If you run under a platform without a shield and get zapped, this will take away some RaggieRage from you! Once the bar is filled, it will light up and you will have Rage allowances (indicated by the circles under the bar). You can now use your selected RaggieRage power!!

What is RaggieRush?
    RaggieRush are summoning superpowers that come with VIP Plushies, or with Raggies that have a plush toy trait. Within RaggieRun, you will need a full RaggieRush bar in order to use these summons. Collecting items worth 2 points or more, or destroying obstacles which award 3 points will fill this bar. Running on platforms will also fill this bar. Unlike the RaggieRage bar, this bar will not deplete over time. However, if you run under platforms without a shield and get zapped, you will lose ALL your RaggieRush!! Once the bar is filled, it will light up and you will have Rush allowances (indicated by the circles under the bar). You can now use your selected RaggieRush power!!

My Raggie has no superpowers. Does that mean I cannot score points by destroying obstacles?
    No! Any Raggie has the ability to RaggieSlide! Pressing the down arrow key will make Raggie slide along the platform for a short time. On mobile, this can be down by tapping the right side of the screen. When your RaggieRage bar is full, sliding into obstacles will destroy them, using up 1 RaggieRage allowance and granting additional points. Sliding into BlockOwls with a full RaggieRage bar will free the owl and score you even more points!

How do I get BlockOwls to spawn in the game?
    If you hold a BlockOwls NFT from any of the main collections - Hydra, Plutus, Tygar and C#1, you will see random BlockOwls spawn when playing RaggieRun. Please note that the Story collection is not supported.

Can the game be paused?
    Yes! Pressing the ESC key will pause the game on desktop. Tapping anywhere in the middle of the screen on mobile will also pause the game! Please note that there is a maximum limit of 30min per pausing session. You must return to the game before this 30min countdown elapses or your game session will terminate automatically.
