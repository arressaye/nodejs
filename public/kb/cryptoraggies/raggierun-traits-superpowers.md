The different traits of our Raggies bring different in-game superpowers. Some of our very rare traits have powerful utility in our game RaggieRun!

Some of the more common toys and neck accessories spawn as in-game pick-ups, granting 2 points when collected. These include bandanas, bowties, capes, collars, collarbells, fishbones, featherwands, kibbles, rocketwands, tennis balls, yarnballs. The plush toy traits will also spawn in-game as items to be picked up. Additionally, they will also unlock access to RaggieRush summons! The rarer items, such as blockchain, ghost chain, Cardano ball, moon ball, gold chain etc will also spawn as items to be picked up. In addition they will also unlock RaggieRage superpowers for your Raggie to score even more points!

RaggieRage
    1. Raggies with laser eyes have access to the laser eyes power (naturally!). Unleash a devastating laser beam, destroying all blocks on screen. Each block zapped scores 3 points. Once this superpower has been used, the Raggie will be engulfed in flames for a few seconds during which running into blocks will destroy them for 10 points, and running into BlockOwls will free them!
    2. Raggies with HUD eyes can unveil a hidden dungeon and bring their Raggie into a mini maze game! Each coin in the dungeon gives 10 points. Find the treasure within the time limit and take away a hefty bonus! Bonus points diminish as time passes in the dungeon!
    3. Raggies with their tongue out can spawn our in-game collab with SushiBytes to summon a large billboard which will destroy all obstacles, and SushiBytes will start to appear. Picking them up will grant 10 points.
    4. Raggies with a pendant necklace can activate a 2x point multiplier to double all points from pick-ups for 10 seconds.
    5. Raggies with a ghost chain can transform briefly into a ghostly apparition, passing through blocks that spawn and gaining a spirit orb for each block passed though (capped at 5). When Raggie gets out of the ghostly state, 5 pulsed attacks will launch, destroying all boxes on screen at each pulse and granting a score multiplier equal to the number of spirit orbs gathered during the ghostly state.
    6. Raggies with a blockchain necklace can activate the Blockchain that fires lasers from the sky destoying blocks and it also sets owls free!
    7. Raggies with a goldchain necklace have the Meowdas Touch! Use the Goldchain Superpower to turn those nasty boxes into gold, collect them to get 10 points each!!
    8. Raggies with a cardano ball can summon thunderclouds and see Cardano balls rain from the sky! Each Cardano ball picked up from the storm grants 10 points. But beware of the boxes and platform lasers when chasing those Cardano balls down!!
    9. Raggies with a moon ball can cause boxes to defy gravity and float off to the moon! At the end of the zero-gravity time, a moon shot lottery will run granting a bonus score! By default, this lottery will grant a 2-digit bonus score but there is a 1 in 5 chance of having a 3rd digit spawned! Each digit is randomised up to the total number of boxes "mooned", so theoretically it is possible to get 999 bonus points!!!
    10. Raggies with an alonzo hard fork toy can enable a passive shield which stays on until it is expended. May sound underwhelming but indeed a real life-saver! Also while the shield is active, Raggie will be immune to platform zaps. Instead each zap grants a small amount of Rage and Rush.
    11. Raggies with a smart contract toy that will grant you a random Rage power regardless of whether you actually have the trait or not. Press SPACE to use the random Rage power.

RaggieRush
    Raggies with bull, bear, monkey, whale, hydra or ape plush toys can summon the corresponding animal (or beast!), which will destroy all blocks in its vicinity. Each destroyed block grants 3 points. Different animals feature different animations, discover them all! Players who hold these VIP Plushie NFTs will also gain access to these RaggieRush superpowers, even with a Nakie! (A Nakie is a Raggie with no toys and no neck accessories)

Passive Superpowers
    1. Raggies with lilac coat  have a passive superpower! Every minute, there is a 50% chance of Lilac being activated. When activated, your Raggie will emit a glow, and will be invincible to obstacles for 10 seconds. Owls can be freed with Lilac, and will grant 3 points for the crash instead of the usual 1 point!
    2. Raggies with a gradient background will receive a speed boost when entering the boss fight for quicker movement to dodge attacks.
    3. Raggies with a special background such as climate change, moon and pride will receive a speed boost and the ability to quick drop during the boss fight for even more agility evading the bosses attacks.

Nakies
    A Raggie with no neck accessory and no toys is known as a Nakie. With considerably less ways to score points, this hardcore Raggie allows you to compete on the dedicated Nakie*Run Leaderboard amongst the most patient and dedicated RaggieRun players.
