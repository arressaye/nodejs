#Company Background
Ragsters Productions is a web3 development company founded in 2020 focusing mainly on developing smart contract solutions for decentralised applications. We provide bespoke services to companies and projects, building out platforms and solutions to their exact requirements, powered by our proprietary web2 and web3 based technologies.
We are also a leader in the space of AI application, incorporating AI and RAG technologies into a wide range of tools and solutions that we build.

#Development Timescale Estimates
A typical custom marketplace will require around 20-50 hours of development time.

A typical minting site will require 8-20 hours of development time.

We can also develop other full-stack ecosystem platforms as required by your project and the development timescale depends on the specific complexity of the platform you need. Book an appointment with us to discuss your requirements and get a personalised quote.

Clients will be charged a flat fee for the dev hours and also ongoing maintenance and usage fees as detailed under "Pricing".

This is only a very rough estimate of the time required. Specific requirements for some projects may significantly lengthen the development timescale and incur additional costs as a result. It is the client's responsibility to understand and factor in the possibility of rising costs during the development period if the scope of work changes after a quote has been issued.
